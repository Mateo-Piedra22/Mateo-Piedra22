## ⚡ About Me

I am a **Computer Engineering student** at the *National University of the Littoral (UNL)* in Argentina and a **Full-Stack & Systems Developer** focused on building high-performance, developer-first tooling, local-first architectures, desktop applications, and agentic AI systems.

- 🎓 **Education**: Studying Computer Engineering at *Universidad Nacional del Litoral (UNL)*, Argentina.
- 🚀 **Currently Building**: [**Cline Marketplace**](https://github.com/Mateo-Piedra22/ClineMarket) — developer-grade local control plane for the Cline agent ecosystem.
- 🛠️ **Founding & Leading**: [**MotionA**](https://motiona.xyz) — crafting modern digital experiences, systems, and creative technology.
- 💡 **Core Interests**: Distributed Systems, High-Concurrency Tooling, Local-First Storage, Reverse Engineering, and Agentic AI Architecture (MCP).
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Node.js runtime internals, TypeScript, Express architecture, desktop app design, and systems engineering.
